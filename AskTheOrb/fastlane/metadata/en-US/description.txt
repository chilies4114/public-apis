The fortune-telling toy you grew up with, with one honest twist: you can see the odds, and you can change them.

Ask a yes-or-no question, shake your phone, and the orb answers. Every reading also shows you the number behind it — the actual chance of yes that produced that answer — so you always know whether the orb was leaning your way or not.

THE CLASSIC TWENTY
All twenty original answers, drawn on the original odds: ten yes, five maybe, five no. Shake to ask, or tap the orb. Haptics and sound land with the reveal.

THE ORB REMEMBERS
Ask the same question twice in a day and you'll get the same answer. No re-rolling until you like what you hear — unless you deliberately ask again.

SEE THE ODDS
Every reading comes with a percentage and a scale showing where it landed between no, maybe and yes. Nothing is hidden.

SOME QUESTIONS IT WON'T ANSWER
Questions about self-harm, violence, abuse or medical care get support resources instead of a random verdict. That behaviour is built in and cannot be switched off.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS IS
Ask the Orb is a toy. Answers are drawn at random from the odds you choose. It cannot predict the future, and nothing it says is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. A disclaimer to that effect is shown on first launch and stays available in Settings.
