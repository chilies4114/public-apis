The fortune-telling toy you grew up with, with one honest twist: it shows you the exact odds behind every answer — and nobody, including us, can change them.

Ask a yes-or-no question, shake your phone, and the orb answers. Every reading shows the real chance it was drawn on: 10 in 20. Not an estimate — a count of the answers in the pool.

THE CLASSIC TWENTY
All twenty original answers, drawn on the original odds: ten yes, five maybe, five no. Shake to ask, or tap the orb. Haptics and sound land with the reveal.

THE ORB REMEMBERS
Ask the same question twice in a day and you'll get the same answer. No re-rolling until you like what you hear — unless you deliberately ask again.

THE ODDS ARE A MEASUREMENT, NOT A SETTING
The orb picks one answer at random from its pool, so the chance of a yes is simply how many answers say yes: 10 of 20. There is no dial to turn. Every pack has the same 10 / 5 / 5 shape, so buying one changes what the orb says and provably not what it decides.

SOME QUESTIONS IT WON'T ANSWER
Questions about self-harm, violence, abuse or medical care get support resources instead of a random verdict. That behaviour is built in and cannot be switched off.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS IS
Ask the Orb is a toy. Answers are drawn at random from a fixed pool. It cannot predict the future, and nothing it says is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. A disclaimer to that effect is shown on first launch and stays available in Settings.
