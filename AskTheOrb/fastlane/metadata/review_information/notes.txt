Ask the Orb is an entertainment app — a fortune-telling orb that shows the
probability behind each answer.

HOW TO TEST THE FREE TIER
Launch the app, type any yes-or-no question, and tap "Ask the Orb" (or shake
the device). The free tier allows 5 questions per calendar day. On the 6th
attempt the subscription screen is presented.

HOW TO TEST THE SUBSCRIPTION
The paywall is reachable at any time via "Go Pro" in the top-right of the Ask
tab, or by tapping any locked preset on the Odds tab. Three products are
offered: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial,
and a one-time lifetime unlock. Price, billing period, renewal terms and links
to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are all shown on the same screen as the
purchase button. "Restore" is in the top-right of that screen and also in
Settings.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
There is no sign-in of any kind. Entitlement is read from
Transaction.currentEntitlements, so a sandbox purchase unlocks Pro immediately
and a reinstall restores it without any user action.

DATA COLLECTION
None. Questions, readings and notes are stored only on the device. There is no
analytics SDK, no network calls other than StoreKit, and no tracking.

SAFETY SCREENING
Before any answer is drawn, the question is screened on device against a list
of sensitive topics. Questions touching self-harm, violence, abuse or medical
care are declined outright: no reading is generated, no daily ask is consumed,
nothing is written to history, and the user is shown support resources
(988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, findahelpline.com, and the
National Domestic Violence Hotline as appropriate). Legal and money questions
still receive an answer but carry a standing "not advice" warning.

To verify: type "should I kill myself" and tap Ask. No verdict is produced.
The screening runs entirely on device; no question is ever transmitted.

DISCLAIMER
A full disclaimer is presented as a gate on first launch and must be
acknowledged before the app can be used; it cannot be swiped away. It remains
available at any time under Settings > Disclaimer, and a short form is shown in
the Settings About card and the App Store description.
