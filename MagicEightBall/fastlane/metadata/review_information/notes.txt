Eight Ball Oracle is an entertainment app — a Magic 8 Ball that shows the
probability behind each answer.

HOW TO TEST THE FREE TIER
Launch the app, type any yes-or-no question, and tap "Ask the Ball" (or shake
the device). The free tier allows 5 questions per calendar day. On the 6th
attempt the subscription screen is presented.

HOW TO TEST THE SUBSCRIPTION
The paywall is reachable at any time via "Go Pro" in the top-right of the Ask
tab, or by tapping any locked preset on the Odds tab. Three products are
offered: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial,
and a one-time lifetime unlock. Price, billing period, renewal terms and links
to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are all shown on the same screen as the
purchase button. "Restore" is in the top-right of that screen and also in
Settings.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
There is no sign-in of any kind. Entitlement is read from
Transaction.currentEntitlements, so a sandbox purchase unlocks Pro immediately
and a reinstall restores it without any user action.

DATA COLLECTION
None. Questions, readings and notes are stored only on the device. There is no
analytics SDK, no network calls other than StoreKit, and no tracking.

DISCLAIMER
The app states plainly in Settings and in the App Store description that it is
a toy, that answers are random, and that it should not be used for medical,
legal or financial decisions.
